## Central claim

Structural Flow does not obtain structure for free.

Successful passages establish real capacities:

**available → discriminated → inherited → incorporated**

but no successful structural passage acquires a stronger guarantee merely because it succeeded.

In particular:

**structural success is not, by itself, structural immunity.**

Any stronger guarantee requires its own warrant.

## What closes?

At the scope declared in the manuscript:

* Chance is least sufficient under commitment inclusion for the declared primitive task;
* the primitive signature closes with six non-optional faces;
* incorporated carried consequence requires four distinct foundational capacities;
* those capacities remain structurally separable;

Closure therefore does not mean metaphysical finality. It means that no unresolved structural remainder survives at the declared scope under the accepted definitions.

## What is this not?

This work does not claim:

* that later structural success is guaranteed;
* that every downstream Structural Flow object has already been derived;
* that Structural Entropy is thermodynamic entropy;
* that the five foundational failure regimes exhaust every downstream failure signature;
* that formal consistency establishes empirical truth;
* that Lean validates Structural Flow empirically;
* or that the formal vocabulary used here is the only possible representation of the underlying objects.
